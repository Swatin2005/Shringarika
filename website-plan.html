Organisational Behaviour · Fourth Assignment · Website project plan

Website Planning & Assessment

Business: Shringarika — jewellery rental store serving customers around Gangapur Road, Nashik, Maharashtra.

Website purpose: Introduce the store, display jewellery categories, attract relevant local customers and convert visitors into enquiries through WhatsApp.

Assessment areaHow the Shringarika website addresses it
1. Purpose and target audience (4 marks)The website promotes jewellery rental for weddings, engagements, receptions, festivals and parties. Its target audience includes brides, family members, wedding guests and people seeking occasion jewellery in Nashik, especially around Gangapur Road. The site supports business goals by building awareness, showcasing styles and encouraging enquiries/bookings.
2. Content and information architecture (4 marks)Information is arranged into Home, About, Collections, Featured Rentals, How It Works and Contact. The navigation uses clear section names. Product cards describe sample styles and direct visitors to enquire. The contact form collects only details needed to start an enquiry. The footer repeats important links and location information.
3. User experience and visual design (4 marks)The design uses a consistent burgundy, blush, cream and gold palette with serif headings and readable body text. Jewellery and bridal images make the business easy to understand. Cards, buttons, whitespace, responsive layouts and clear calls to action support easy browsing on phones and computers. Images have descriptive alternative text and controls have visible keyboard focus.
4. Technical requirements (4 marks)The site is built as a lightweight, responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript website. It can be hosted on a static hosting platform. A domain such as shringarika.in may be registered if available; availability must be checked. HTTPS should be enabled through the hosting provider. Images are stored locally in the images folder. The WhatsApp number must be configured before launch. Test loading speed and mobile layout before publishing.
5. SEO and digital marketing (3 marks)The page includes a descriptive title, meta description, relevant local keywords such as “jewellery on rent Nashik” and “Gangapur Road jewellery rental”, and social-sharing metadata. Helpful original product descriptions, real store photos, a verified Google Business Profile, Instagram posts, customer reviews with consent and consistent business contact details can improve local discovery.
6. Security, privacy and accessibility (3 marks)The current enquiry form opens WhatsApp and does not store form entries on the website. The site does not request passwords, payment card details or customer accounts. Use HTTPS, keep hosting and domain accounts protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and publish a privacy notice before collecting personal information. The site uses semantic sections, form labels, image alt text, responsive text and keyboard focus indicators. Avoid collecting unnecessary customer data.
7. Testing, maintenance and evaluation (2 marks)Before launch, test all navigation links, WhatsApp buttons, form fields, image loading, spelling, mobile and desktop layouts, keyboard navigation and HTTPS. Update stock, photos, prices, rental duration, deposit and policies regularly. Evaluate performance using enquiries, WhatsApp clicks, visitor traffic, popular collections and completed bookings.
8. Organization, presentation and references (1 mark)The website and this project plan use clear headings, consistent design and concise business information. References below support accessibility, SEO and performance recommendations. Actual store details, photographs, prices and customer testimonials should be verified before publication.

Launch checklist

  • Replace the WhatsApp placeholder in index.html with the store's official number.
  • Replace sample images with original photos of actual rental jewellery wherever possible.
  • Confirm the exact shop address, opening hours and appointment process.
  • Add actual rental prices, security deposit, rental duration, late-return and damage policies only after the business confirms them.
  • Publish a privacy notice and clear rental terms.
  • Test the site on Android, iPhone, tablet and desktop before sharing it publicly.
